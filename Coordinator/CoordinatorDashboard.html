Course Offering Rules

  • Choose an existing course, a listed semester, and a listed faculty member.
  • Use a section letter with an optional digit, such as A or B1.
  • Available seats must be a whole number from 1 to 60.
  • The same course, semester, and section cannot be added twice.
  • A faculty member cannot use the same predefined time slot twice in one semester.
  • These are fictional teaching rules, not an official AIUB calendar or room-allocation system.
